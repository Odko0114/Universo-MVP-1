How we handle data

Universo is built so a university can use it without ever receiving personal data about a student who hasn't chosen to contact them. Partner analytics are aggregate counts only — there is no screen anywhere in the product that shows you an individual student.

  • What we collect: anonymous engagement events (profile views, saves, apply clicks, searches) tied to a random client id stored in a first-party cookie — never a name or email.
  • What partners can see: aggregate counts and trends for your own institution only — views, saves, apply clicks, and viewer countries. Never an individual student, and never another institution's numbers.
