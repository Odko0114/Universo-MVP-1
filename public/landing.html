Why we built this

"Choosing a university abroad is a one-shot decision — most people never get a do-over. And right now that shot goes to whoever has an agent, a counselor, or an older sibling who already made it. We came to Finland as international students ourselves, then spent months talking to every one of Finland's 37 higher-education institutions. The pattern was always the same: students with someone to ask landed somewhere right for them, and students without were guessing. Universo exists to close that gap — same information, same tools, same chance, whoever you are."

— Odko & Mir, founders of Universo
