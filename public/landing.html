Good questions

Everything you're probably wondering

Is Universo really free?

Yes — free forever, with no card and no trial. Universities support the platform so that students never have to pay to find where they belong.

Is the data accurate?

Every university is drawn from ETER, the EU's official register. On top of that, 300 have deeply verified profiles we've checked by hand. When we show a number, it comes from a real source.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. You can match and browse every university without signing up. An account simply lets you save your shortlist, compare side by side, and pick up where you left off.

How is this different from searching myself?

A search gives you links. Universo matches universities to what actually fits you, tells you why in plain words, and puts the real data — tuition, deadlines, language — side by side so you can decide with confidence.

Which countries does it cover?

3,835 universities across 32 European countries — from Finland to Spain, Ireland to Italy.
